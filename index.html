New Mexico

New Mexico is a landlocked state in the Southwestern United States, officially nicknamed the "Land of Enchantment". It is bordered by Colorado to the north, Oklahoma and Texas to the east and northeast, Arizona to the west, and Mexico (Chihuahua and Sonora) to the south. New Mexico has a deep, multi-layered tri-cultural identity blending Indigenous, Hispanic/Spanish, and Anglo traditions. The capital of the state is Sata Fe and the largest city is Alburquerque.
